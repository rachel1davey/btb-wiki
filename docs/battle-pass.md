# Battle Pass

Run `/battlepass` to see the season, challenges, tiers, and rewards.

## Progress and rewards

Complete challenges to earn progress and unlock rewards. New quests arrive each week. A season runs for seven weeks. The Battle Pass menu shows the dates and your progress.

Everyone can earn Free tier rewards, including Gems, money, and Vote Crate keys. The Premium tier adds cosmetics, keys, Gems, and money.

The Premium Battle Pass costs 2,000 Gems. Check the in game shop before buying in case the price has changed.

Claim rewards in the Battle Pass menu. A Battle Pass experience booster helps you earn progress faster. Check its description for the multiplier and duration.
