# Gems, Crates and Keys

## Gems

Gems are a separate in game currency. Buy them from the [BackToBlocks store](https://store.backtoblocks.fun) or earn them in the Battle Pass. Use Gems for selected crate keys and cosmetic trades.

The Cosmetics Market lets players trade cosmetics with Gems. Open the market in game to see the listings.

## Crate keys

The Key Shop lists these prices:

* Tag Crate Key: 100 Gems
* Monthly Cosmetic Crate Key: 300 Gems
* Monthly Plushie Crate Key: 300 Gems

Vote Crate keys can drop when you vote. Check Discord's `#vote-key` channel for the voting link. The Battle Pass also gives keys.

## Crates

* **Vote Crate:** Rewards include money, useful items, tags, and custom enchantments.
* **Tag Crate:** Player tags.
* **Monthly Cosmetic Crate:** Rotating cosmetics. These cosmetics will not be sold again.
* **Monthly Plushie Crate:** Plushie cosmetics.
* **Enchants Crates:** Green costs $8,000, Red costs $12,500, and Pink costs $25,000. Each crate contains books from its tier. See the [Custom Enchant Crates guide](custom-enchant-crates.md).
