# How to Trade with Players

Updated Oct 27, 2026

Use `/trade` to swap items without dropping them on the ground.

## Start a trade

1. Stand near the player you want to trade with.
2. Run `/trade <player>`. For example: `/trade Steve`.
3. The other player must accept the request.

## Complete the trade

1. Put your items in your side of the trade menu.
2. Check the other player's offer.
3. Both players must accept to finish the trade.

If either player changes an item, both players must accept again. Check the offers before you confirm.

## Trade safely

* Check enchantments and item counts before accepting.
* Use `/trade` instead of dropping valuable items.
* If you do not know the other player, trade in a public area.

For a trade dispute, open a Discord ticket with screenshots, the player's name, and the approximate time.
