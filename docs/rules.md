# Rules

These rules apply to everyone on BackToBlocks. Staff handle situations not covered here to protect the community and keep play fair.

## Respect other players

Bullying, personal attacks, hate speech, slurs, offensive content, excessive spam, and impersonating staff or players are not allowed. Do not set homes at another player's base.

## No cheating or exploits

Hacked clients, X ray packs or mods, duping, and abusing glitches are not allowed. Report bugs instead of exploiting them. Cheating can lead to serious punishments.

## No griefing, raiding, stealing, or TP trapping

Griefing, raiding, and stealing are not allowed. TP trapping is prohibited in any form.

## PvP

Everyone can use `/pvp toggle`. PvP only takes place between players who have it turned on. Turning it on means you agree to fight other players who have PvP on too.

## Respect community areas

You need graylist access to build in protected community areas. Do not build near another player's base without permission or block public paths, portals, or entrances. Follow staff instructions during community projects.

## Use fair mods and tools

Helpful mods are allowed, but unfair advantages are not. Do not use minimaps to find bases, players, or hidden structures. Building tools are for planning and building, not automated printing or scouting bases. Automated pathfinding and movement bots are not allowed.

## Use accounts and farms responsibly

Each player can use one alternate account. Do not use it to get around a punishment. Keep server performance in mind when running AFK farms. Staff can limit or remove farms that cause lag.

By playing here, you agree to follow these rules. Staff will step in when needed to protect the community and keep play fair.
