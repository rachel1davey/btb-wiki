# Custom Enchant Crates

Pick a crate by enchantment tier. Each opening gives you a book from that tier.

* **Green, Tier 1:** Common enchantments. $8,000 per opening.
* **Red, Tier 2:** Rare enchantments. $12,500 per opening.
* **Pink, Tier 3:** Epic enchantments. $25,000 per opening.

Buy an opening in `/shop`, then use `/keys` to preview or open it. Right click a crate in the key menu to preview it. Left click to open it.

## Enchantment levels

Higher levels are harder to get than lower levels in the same tier. For example, Angelic II has a lower chance than Angelic I. The preview shows each book and its chance.
