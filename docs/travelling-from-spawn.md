# Travelling from spawn

Use `/wild` to leave spawn and travel into the wilderness. You can use it during your first 24 hours of playtime or your first seven days after joining, whichever comes first.

Build at least 700 blocks from spawn so you have room to explore and build. Once you have settled in, use `/sethome` to save your location.

## PvP

Everyone can turn PvP on or off with `/pvp toggle`. You can fight players whose PvP is on. Turning yours on means you agree to take part in PvP.
