# Shopping District and Shops

Updated Oct 27, 2026

The Shopping District is where players set up shops and sell goods to each other.

## Buy a plot

1. Find an available plot in the Shopping District.
2. Use `/plot buy`.
3. Build your shop inside the plot boundaries.

Use `/shop` to see options for more shop space. Players can visit with `/plot visit` or travel to the district.

## Shop guidelines

* Keep your build inside your plot.
* Leave paths, entrances, and nearby shops clear.
* Keep redstone and entities under control.
* Do not use a shop to trap other players.

Staff can remove abandoned shops or builds that cause problems.
