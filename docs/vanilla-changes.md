# Vanilla Changes

BackToBlocks keeps the usual survival feel and adds a few changes to gameplay and building.

## Gameplay and performance

* Dragon damage tracking shows who hit the Ender Dragon.
* Mob spawn rates change with the number of players, and each player has an individual mob cap.
* Use `/phantoms toggle` to turn phantoms on or off.
* View distance adjusts to server performance and player count.
* Leaves fall faster after a tree is cut down.
* World generation adds custom terrain and biomes.

## Getting around

* Use `/sethome` to set a home and `/spawn` to return to spawn.
* Voting can unlock some warps.

## Chat and privacy

* Mojang chat reporting is disabled.
* Use `[item]` and `[ender]` in chat to show items and Ender Chest contents.

## Building and decoration

* Sponsor Rank players can change armor stand poses. Everyone can also unlock this through `/voteshop`.
* Invisible item frames and custom player heads are available for builds.
* Custom Rank players can lock maps on signs. This is also available through `/voteshop`.
* Monthly Supporter Rank players can display items on signs.
