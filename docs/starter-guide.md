# Starter Guide

## Start playing

Use `/wild` to leave spawn and find a place to settle. You can use it during your first 24 hours of playtime or your first seven days after joining, whichever comes first. Build at least 700 blocks from spawn to give yourself room. Once you have chosen a spot, set a home with `/sethome`.

## Things to do

* Go fishing, look for rare catches, and join contests.
* Take on quests and challenges for rewards.
* Choose a job, earn money, and level up.
* Join community events and group chats.
* Vote for the server and spend your rewards in the Vote Shop.
* Explore the custom terrain and biomes.
* Play on Java or Bedrock. Proximity voice chat lets you talk with nearby players.

## Shops and money

Use `/plot buy` to buy a plot in the Shopping District and open a player shop. Other players can visit with `/plot visit` or travel there with `/warp shoppingdistrict`. Use `/shop` to buy server items. Jobs, quests, shops, and trading are all ways to earn money.

## Protected places

Spawn, the Nether Hub, and End Island are protected community areas. You need graylist access to build there. Shopping District plots are managed separately.

**Useful commands:** `/fish`, `/shop`, `/voteshop`, `/lottery`, `/sethome`, `/tpa`, `/trade`, `/vote`, `/phantoms toggle`, and `/warp shoppingdistrict`.
