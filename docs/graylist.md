# Graylist

The graylist protects shared community areas. You can explore, build a base, trade, and play normally before joining it.

## Areas that need graylist access

* Spawn
* Nether Hub
* End Island

The Shopping District is separate. Buy and manage plots there with the plot commands.

## How to join

You qualify after one week of playtime, or after three days if an existing player vouches for you. Then open a ticket in Discord's `#create-ticket` channel and send a short application.

Graylist access lets you build in protected community areas and join community projects and events. It does not expire.
