# Technical Information

## Server settings

* Dedicated Ryzen 9 9900K server in a professional datacenter with no shared hosting.
* Difficulty: Hard.
* Mob spawn radius: 12 chunks, or 192 blocks.
* Soft mob despawn radius: 28 blocks from players.
* Hard mob despawn radius: 96 blocks from players.
* Keep Inventory is off except at spawn.
* Fire does not spread.
* Entity cramming limit: 14 entities in one block.

## View and simulation distance

Simulation distance controls how many nearby chunks are active. View distance controls how far you can see. The server adjusts both to help performance.

| World | Simulation distance | View distance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Overworld | 6 to 12 | 8 to 16 |
| Nether | 6 to 10 | 8 to 14 |
| End | 6 to 10 | 8 to 12 |

The Nether and End can have shorter distances. Chunks shared by nearby players count once.

## Mob caps

Mob spawning is counted per player, so the number of players online affects how many mobs can spawn.

* Hostile mobs: 100 per player
* Passive animals: 20 per player
* Water animals: 10 per player
* Water ambient mobs: 20 per player
* Underground water creatures: 15 per player
* Axolotls: 5 per player
* Ambient mobs, including bats: 15 per player

## Performance limits

**Per chunk:** 128 hoppers, 20 armor stands, 15 villagers, 12 End Crystals, and 12 sniffers. The limit is 40 animals and 40 monsters.

**Nearby entity limits:** Up to 40 cows, pigs, sheep, chickens, or turtles within 15 blocks, and up to 40 boats rendered near a player.

Send suggestions or concerns about these limits in Discord.
