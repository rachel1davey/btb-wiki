# Banking and Earning Money

Updated Oct 27, 2026

## Earn money

Jobs and quests pay money and help you progress. At spawn, use the selling NPCs for mob drops, wood, ores, and crops. Their menu shows what they accept and what each item pays.

## Use the bank

Talk to the Banker at spawn to deposit money. The bank pays interest every 20 minutes. Upgrade it with items such as Emeralds and Diamonds to increase your interest. Check the cost and rate before you confirm an upgrade.

## Daily Wheel

Use `/wheel spin` at spawn to spin the Daily Wheel. The menu shows the rewards and when you can spin again.

## Referral rewards

Run `/referral` to see your code and progress. New players can enter a code with `/refer <code>`. When a referral succeeds, both players get one Vote Key.

Referral milestones give extra rewards:

* 5 referrals: 3 Vote Keys
* 10 referrals: 8 Vote Keys
* 25 referrals: 5 Diamond Blocks, 3 Enchanted Golden Apples, an Elytra, and 10 Vote Keys
* 100 referrals: 25 Diamond Blocks, 25 Enchanted Golden Apples, 15 Netherite Ingots, a Beacon, and a Custom Tag Voucher

Use `/referral` to check your progress.
