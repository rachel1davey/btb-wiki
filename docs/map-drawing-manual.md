# Map Drawing Manual

## Open the drawing menu

Run `/draw` or `/paint`. From the menu, you can start a picture, open a draft, import a map, read the manuals, or use the easel. Run the command again while drawing to open the tools.

Stand on a solid block and leave enough clear space around you. Your character stays still while you draw.

## Create or edit a picture

Choose **Create a Painting** to start with a blank canvas. To edit a map, hold it and choose **Import Painting**. Then choose a color, cursor size, background, and drawing tool.

Use **WASD** to move the cursor. Hold **Shift** to move farther, then right click to draw. Press **Q** to undo. The last 15 actions are saved.

Use `/draw save` to save your work. Use `/draw cancel` to leave without saving.

## Commands

* `/draw cursor <size>`: Set the cursor size from 1 to 16.
* `/draw color #HEX_COLOR`: Change the cursor color.
* `/draw background #HEX_COLOR`: Change the canvas background.
* `/draw pencil`, `/draw eraser`, or `/draw fill`: Choose a drawing tool.
* `/draw line`, `/draw square`, `/draw filled_square`, `/draw circle`, or `/draw filled_circle`: Choose a shape.
* `/draw selection` or `/draw text`: Choose the selection or text tool.
* `/draw help`: View command help in game.

## Drafts and community pictures

Your unfinished work stays in drafts. Reopen a draft to edit it, delete it, or submit it for publishing. You can keep up to five drafts and three pictures in your collection.

When you submit a picture, choose its name and collection and check the price and edition options. Staff review pictures before they appear for others. Copying paintings is disabled. Up to three players can draw at once.

## Display a picture on a wall

Choose the easel to make a picture for your build. A display can be up to six maps wide and four maps high, so leave enough room on the wall. Your picture stays up when you finish editing.
