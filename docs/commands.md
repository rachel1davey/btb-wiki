# Commands

Some commands depend on your rank, location, or unlocks. Try `/help` or ask staff if a command does not work.

## Getting around

* `/wild`: Travel into the wilderness. New players have limited access.
* `/spawn`: Return to spawn.
* `/sethome`: Set a home.
* `/tpa`: Ask to teleport to another player.
* `/pw`: Open player warps.
* `/warp shoppingdistrict`: Travel to the Shopping District.

## Gameplay and rewards

* `/pvp toggle`: Turn PvP on or off. Everyone can use this command. PvP is consent based.
* `/phantoms toggle`: Turn phantoms on or off.
* `/fish`: Open the fishing menu.
* `/shop`: Browse items for sale and buy Enchants Crate openings.
* `/keys`: View and open your crate keys.
* `/boosters`: View your boosters and active boosts.
* `/battlepass`: Open the Battle Pass.
* `/wheel spin`: Spin the Daily Wheel at spawn.
* `/referral`: View your referral code and progress.
* `/refer <code>`: Enter a referral code as a new player.

## Trading, shops, and orders

* `/trade <player>`: Send a trade request.
* `/orders`: Request items or fulfil another player's order.
* `/plot buy`: Buy an available Shopping District plot.
* `/plot visit`: Visit a player's shop.
* `/ad`: Advertise a Shopping District plot in chat.

## Locks and access

* `/lock`: Lock a supported block. Each player can lock up to five doors. Signs and sticky pistons are supported too.
* `/locks`: View your locked blocks.
* `/trust <player>`: Give a player access to your locks.
* `/untrust <player>`: Remove a player's access.

## Other

* `/voteshop`: Open the Vote Shop.
* `/lottery`: View lottery options.
* `/vote`: View voting information.
