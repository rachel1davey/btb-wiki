# Fishing Guide

## Get started

Run `/fish` to open the fishing menu. Cast your rod as usual. You can catch special fish as well as regular ones.

## Fishing menu

The menu has the fish shop, fish bag, bait store, fish codex, deliveries, scales, gutting, skills, rod augments, totems, and fishing statistics.

Use the fish shop to sell eligible catches. Keep fish in your fish bag and use the codex to learn about your catches. Deliveries, scales, and statistics each have their own section. Gutting turns eligible fish into fishing resources. Check the confirmation before you continue.

## Bait and progression

Drag bait onto your fishing rod to use it. Its description shows what it does and how many uses remain. The menu shows skill and rod augment requirements before you spend materials. You can also place fishing totems.

## Contests and rare catches

When a fishing contest starts, follow the server announcement and check the fishing menu for details. Some rare catches are announced to everyone.

The fishing menu shows the activities and rewards available now.
